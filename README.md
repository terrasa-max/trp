The real-income computer game TERRAPARK is an analog of the MONOPOLY board game. TERRAPARK consists of 4 international startups: hotels, cafes, a food aggregator, a digital bank with artificial intelligence, a film about the new technology of mankind, a travel show and the production of regional semi-finished products for retailers.

In TERRAPARK, players actually become the "owners" of cities and regions of the Russian Federation, shareholders and business owners in all cities of Russia.

Tokens can be used to pay for shares of joint-stock companies, franchises, licenses, and other products.

Tokens are secured by shares of companies, technologies, and real estate rights.
